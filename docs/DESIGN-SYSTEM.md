# Build 006 visual identity
Original design: cream workspace, white surfaces, mint primary actions, lavender selection, soft pink secondary surfaces and yellow review states. Ink #28323A and substantial outlines preserve contrast. Buttons, cards and panels have rounded corners, restrained shadows and visible keyboard focus. Reduced-motion preferences remove transitions.

Headings use locally bundled Fredoka; controls/body use Nunito. Both font families are SIL Open Font License 1.1; licences and copyright notices ship in public/fonts/fredoka and public/fonts/nunito. Fixed-weight 500 print instances are generated with fontTools by scripts/prepare-print-fonts.py. No paid fonts or image services are used. Lucide provides interface icons. Character/environment tile previews are original project-authored SVG drawings.

UI colours are isolated from the renderer. `colouring-book-bold` outputs white backgrounds and black outlines. Contour categories are 2.25 pt primary, 1.5 pt secondary, 0.8 pt fine. Solution overlays may use monochrome grey and do not colour player pages. Export composition omits every interaction layer and grid.
